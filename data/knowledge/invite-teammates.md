id: kb-invite-teammates
title: Inviting teammates and domain allowlists
category: how_to
component: auth
---

Admins invite teammates from **Workspace settings → Members → Invite**. Invites expire in seven days.

On Team and Enterprise, enable **Allowed email domains** so invites can only go to listed domains (for example `riverbank.test`). Starter workspaces can invite any email but are capped by seat count.
