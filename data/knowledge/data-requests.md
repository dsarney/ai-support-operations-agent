id: kb-data-requests
title: Data deletion, export, and legal requests
category: legal
component: auth
---

GDPR erasure, CCPA deletion, litigation holds, and bulk data export are legal operations. The agent must never delete accounts, wipe backups, or email personal data to a ticket.

Escalate to a human with the customer plan, requester email, and exact wording of the request. Do not promise a completion date in the automated reply.
