title: Invoices, failed charges, and refunds
category: billing
component: billing
---

Monthly invoices appear under **Billing → Invoices**. `paid`, `open`, and `failed` are the only statuses in this demo.

Failed charges can be retried by the customer from the billing portal. Refunds, credits, plan downgrades, and chargebacks are **not** automated. Escalate billing disputes and refund demands to a human with the invoice IDs attached.
